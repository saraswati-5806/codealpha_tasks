Lesson 02

Email Red Flags

Phishing emails often contain warning signs. Learning these red flags helps you stop attacks before clicking anything.

From: support@paypa1-security.com
Subject: Your account will be suspended in 24 hours!

Dear Customer,

Your account has unusual activity. Click the link below immediately to verify your details.

https://paypal.com.verify-login-security.ru

Please download the attached verification form and submit your password.

Fake Email Inspector

Red flags: suspicious sender, urgent threat, generic greeting, fake link, and dangerous attachment request.

01

Wrong Sender

Look for similar but incorrect domains like paypa1.com instead of paypal.com.

02

Urgent Language

“Act now” and “24 hours left” are used to create panic.

03

Generic Greeting

Phishing emails often say “Dear Customer” instead of your real name.

04

Suspicious Links

Displayed text may look safe, but the actual link may lead elsewhere.

05

Unexpected Attachments

Be careful with .exe, .zip, and macro-enabled documents.

06

Poor Grammar

Many phishing emails contain spelling mistakes and unnatural wording.
