Lesson 05

Best Practices

The best protection against phishing is awareness, verification, and safe digital habits.

01

Enable MFA

Multi-Factor Authentication protects accounts even if passwords leak.

02

Don’t Click Blindly

Type official URLs manually instead of clicking unknown links.

03

Verify Identity

Call the person or company using an official contact method.

04

Password Manager

Use unique passwords and avoid reuse across websites.

05

Update Devices

Keep your browser, OS, and apps updated to fix known vulnerabilities.

06

Report Phishing

Use Gmail/Outlook report phishing options to protect others.

Golden Rule

Pause

When in doubt, do not open. Delete the message and verify through official channels.
