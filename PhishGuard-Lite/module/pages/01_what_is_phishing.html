Lesson 01

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers pretend to be trusted people or companies to trick users into sharing passwords, money, OTPs, or sensitive information.

Steal Passwords

Attackers create fake login pages to capture usernames, passwords, and OTPs.

Steal Money

Fake bank alerts, payment links, and refund scams trick victims into transferring money.

Install Malware

Attachments or downloads may install malicious software on the victim’s device.

01

Email Phishing

Mass emails sent to many people pretending to be banks, companies, or services.

02

Spear Phishing

Personalized phishing targeting one person or organization using specific details.

03

Smishing

Phishing through SMS or messaging apps using fake delivery, bank, or reward links.

04

Vishing

Voice-call scams where attackers pretend to be support, bank, or government staff.

05

Whaling

Phishing attacks targeting executives, managers, or people with high authority.

Tip

Simple Analogy

Phishing is like a fisherman casting a net and waiting for someone to bite.
