Lesson 03

Fake Websites

Attackers clone real websites to steal login details. A fake page may look real, but the URL usually reveals the truth.

https://www.amazon.com/login

Real Website Pattern

Correct spelling, official domain, normal login flow, no unnecessary urgency.

https://arnazon-login-secure.ru

Fake Website Pattern

Typosquatting domain, fake branding, forced login, and suspicious extension.

01

Typosquatting

Attackers use domains like g00gle.com, paypa1.com, or arnazon.com.

02

HTTPS Myth

A padlock only means encrypted connection, not that the website is trustworthy.

03

Domain Check

Always check the main domain before entering passwords.

04

WHOIS Check

Very new domains can be suspicious when pretending to be large companies.

05

Login Trap

Fake login pages collect usernames, passwords, and OTPs.

06

Visual Clone

Fake pages can copy logos and layouts, so URL checking is essential.
