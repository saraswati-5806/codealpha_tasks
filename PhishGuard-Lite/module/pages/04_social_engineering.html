Lesson 04

Social Engineering Tactics

Social engineering means manipulating people psychologically instead of directly hacking systems.

01

Urgency

“Act now or lose access” forces rushed decisions.

02

Authority

Attackers pretend to be CEOs, managers, banks, or IT support.

03

Fear

Fake virus alerts or account warnings scare victims into action.

04

Curiosity

“You have a parcel” or “See attached photo” tricks users into clicking.

05

Reciprocity

Attackers offer a gift or favor first, then request sensitive information.

Case

John Podesta Hack

A phishing email led to compromised email access during the 2016 election period.
